## 3. Payline Definitions

**20 paylines** `[VERIFIED]` — every captured response reports `betLines: 20`, and payline
indices 1, 10, 13 and 19 have been observed. The layout (which cells each line covers) is still
undocumented. What the docs state, all of it now corroborated by the payloads:

- Evaluation direction: "All symbols pay from left to right on adjacent reels starting from
  the leftmost reel, except Freespin and P1 which pay for any reel" (Game Rules).
- Per-line win formula: **Per Line winnings = (base bet / betLines) × payoutRate** `[VERIFIED]`.
  The full round payout is `randomMultiplier × Σ payoutRate × baseStake / betLines` — exact on
  all five samples (bet-response.md §5). The doc's `/5` worked examples are wrong. The base is
  `baseStake`, NOT the ante-inflated `stake` — proven by the ante round (§4).
- Scatter win formula: **Scatter winnings = base bet × Multiplier** (Game Rules).
- A "Winning Bet Line" illustration exists as embedded image `image15` (Features §9), but it is
  a picture only — no payline coordinate table accompanies it.
- Runtime source of truth: the bridge's `getPaylineCount()` returns `gameInfo.betlines`,
  described as "a **configured fact**, never a per-spin interpretation" (test-bridge-api.md).

Full payout formulas are in [paytable.md](paytable.md).

## 4. Mechanics

### Confirmed Mechanics

**Free Spin** — vs `shared-mechanisms/free-spin.md`: **Deviates**

- How it works here: Bonus on **3+ distinct reels** triggers; the award is
  **base(reels) × ways** — base 12/15/20 for 3/4/5 reels, times the product of the per-reel
  Bonus counts `[VERIFIED: 3 reels, ways 4 → 48 free spins]`. Every free-spin payout is multiplied by that round's Freespin Multiplier
  (x1, x2, x5, x10). Raining Wild is active during free spins. The symbol distribution list is
  reselected **every spin** during free spins (Features §7). Re-trigger is supported
  `[QA-CONFIRMED]`. Bridge support: `awaitFreespinRound(timeoutMs = 60000)` — "passive — the
  client fires each free spin itself"; `HAS_FREE_SPINS | true`.
- Comparison — deviations from the shared pattern:
  1. **Per-round win multiplier**, absent from the shared pattern: "Every free spin payout
     during Free Game are multiplied by the respective Freespin Multiplier(x1,x2,x5,x10) ,
     enhancing potential rewards." (Game Rules)
  2. **Buy-bonus entry path (shared Pattern F) does not apply**: "***The game does not have a
     Buy Bonus.***" (Features §3). Shared Pattern F must be skipped for this game.
  3. **Raining Wild plays inside the bonus**: "During Free Spin, it have raining wild features.
     Raining Wild Animation is a visual effect. So this animation does not influence or alter
     the mathematical outcome of the game." (Features §3)
  4. **The award is REEL-based and multiplied by ways** `[VERIFIED 2026-08-24]` — see
     `games/chaos/bet-response.md` §6. `reels` = distinct reels holding a Bonus (3+ triggers);
     `base` = 12/15/20 for 3/4/5 reels; `award = base x product of the per-reel Bonus counts`.
     A real payload with Bonus counts `[1,2,0,2,0]` -> 3 reels, ways 4, base 12 -> **48 free
     spins**, exactly what the server awarded.
  5. **Both of the doc's readings are wrong.** By total symbol count that grid holds 5 Bonuses and
     would award 20; by the doc's "Base Free Spins (3 reels): 15" it would award 60. The server
     said 48. QA's "12, 15 or 20" was right but keys on **REELS, not symbols**, and the doc's
     ways-multiplier section had the right shape with the wrong base (12, not 15) — its worked
     example `15 x 4 = 60` should read `12 x 4 = 48`.
  6. **The trigger condition is 3+ distinct REELS, not 3+ symbols.** Two Bonuses stacked on one
     reel plus one on another does NOT trigger.
- Undocumented / assumed from the shared pattern (verify before asserting): bet locking during
  free spins, non-deduction of balance per free spin, exact counter-decrement behaviour,
  round-end accumulated-win display, and the **re-trigger award amount** (re-trigger is
  confirmed to exist, but how many spins it grants is not documented anywhere).

**Auto Spin** — vs `shared-mechanisms/auto-spin.md`: **Matches**

- How it works here: evidenced by the bridge only — the design doc never mentions autoplay.
  `HAS_AUTOPLAY | true | btn_autoplay`; popup controls `autoplay:` `confirm` `stop` `cancel`
  `next` `back` `stopAtWin` `close` `count:<i>` (test-bridge-api.md, Control names).
- Comparison: Follows the shared pattern with no behavioural difference on the evidence
  available. Stop-on-win (`stopAtWin`), fixed counts (`count:<i>`), manual stop (`stop`) and a
  configuration popup are all present as the canonical pattern requires. Bonus suspension is
  corroborated by the settle contract: `waitForSpinClickable()` "never resolves during autoplay
  or free spins", and `mode:AUTOPLAY` is a distinct blocking reason.
- Gap (not a deviation): the available spin-count values, and whether an infinite/unlimited
  option exists, are documented nowhere in the source corpus.

**Buy Bonus** — **Absent.** Not applicable; no comparison performed. Two independent explicit
denials: "***The game does not have a Buy Bonus.***" (19_Chaos_runner.md, Features §3) and
`HAS_BUY_BONUS | false | no buy-bonus control exists — buyBonusNode is unwired and
ControlView.handleClickBuyBonus() is empty` (test-bridge-api.md). The control name `buyBonus`
"always resolves to `null`".

**Hold & Spin** — **Absent.** Not applicable; no comparison performed. No coin symbol, re-spin
counter, locked position, or Minor/Major/Grand jackpot tier appears in either source file. The
bridge's optional `holdSpinStart()` / `holdSpinEnd()` methods and `hold_spin_start` /
`hold_spin_end` events are the **press-and-hold spin-button gesture**, not the Hold & Spin
coin-collection bonus `[QA-CONFIRMED]`.

### Custom Mechanics (Game-Specific)

**Ante Bet** — `HAS_ANTE_BET | true | btn_anteBet`; controls `anteBet`, `anteBetInfoClose`.

- Ante Bet Multiplier = **3** `[VERIFIED]` — a real payload reports `anteMultiplier: 3` with
  `baseStake: 0.6` and `stake: 1.8`. Activated per spin, "configurable via JSON" (Game Rules).
- **The payout base does NOT move** `[VERIFIED]`: that round paid `150 × 0.6/20 = 4.5`. Had the
  payout followed the inflated stake it would have paid 13.5. Ante buys a better chance (more
  Wilds — that round landed three), not a bigger payout per line.
- **base bet vs bet amount** (Game Logic, Explanation of terms): *base bet* is "the amount used
  for payout calculation" and the base for bet-per-line and the paytable; *bet amount* is "the
  amount deducted from the player's balance" and "displayed to the player on the UI". In Normal
  Mode base bet = bet amount. Under Ante Bet they diverge: bet amount = base bet × 3, while
  **bet per line and all payouts still derive from the unchanged base bet**. This is the key
  testable property of ante bet in this game.
- Effect on gameplay: "Activating the Ante-Bet will increase the chance to win raining wild
  feature in MainGame and FreeGame Bonus. More Wild symbols are present on the reels."
- Implementation: "Ante Bet is configured to use a specific list selected from those existing
  Normal Bet lists (e.g., List 3)." See Multiple Distribution List below.

**Raining Wild** (Features §1) — appears in **both** Main Game and Bonus Game.

- Trigger: "Before the final results are displayed to the player, if the outcome already
  contains 2 or more Wild symbols, the system may play the Raining Wild animation."
- Probability table: 2 or more Wilds → **100%**; less than 2 Wilds → **0%**.
- Timing: "The Raining Wild animation plays while the reels are still spinning. Once the
  animation completes, the reels proceed to stop and show the final result."
- **Critical for testing:** "This is a visual effect only. The animation does not influence or
  alter the mathematical outcome of the game. Win evaluation is always based solely on the
  originally generated result." Assert this against the raw `BetResponse`, never as a payout
  effect.
- Illustrated by embedded images `image11`, `image12` ("Raining Wild Sequence").

**Random Multiplier / Multiplier Ladder** (Features §2) — `HAS_MULTIPLIER_LADDER | true`.

- "During each spin in the Main Game, the system randomly selects a multiplier either **x1**,
  **x2**, **x5**, **x10**."
- The same multiplier set applies to free spins as the Freespin Multiplier.
- Bridge: `getMultiplierLevels(): { values: number[] }` — "the configured lucky-multiplier
  ladder (`LUCKY_MULTIPLIER_VALUES`) shown by `MulitplierBarView`… a **configured fact**, never
  a per-spin interpretation." Use it to read the ladder, not the current spin's multiplier.
- Illustrated by embedded images `image13`, `image14`.

**P1 Ways-Based Symbol** (Features §4) — UI name **"Fuel"**, symbol id **5** `[VERIFIED]`,
configured as a **SCATTER** (`Scatters: [{Symbol: P1, Occurrences: 3, Multiplier: 10}]`).

- **Verified in live play**: P1 landed `[2,0,1,0,1]` — reels 1, 3, 5 — for `2 x 1 x 1 = 2` ways,
  a reported `scatterMultiplier` of 20, and `rawPayout` 12 = `2 x 10 x baseStake 0.6`. The design
  doc's worked example is reproduced exactly.
- **A scatter pays on the whole bet — no `/betLines` divisor.** This is where the doc is right and
  its line arithmetic is wrong.
- The "Normal (Exclude wild)" in the Symbols Mapping table and the "Scatter type" in Reel Glow
  describe different things and are both correct: Wild cannot substitute for P1, *and* P1 pays by
  occurrence rather than along a payline. Only the doc's "Gun" naming is wrong.

- "Available in both **Base Game** and **Bonus Game**"; "Appears only on reels **1, 3, 5**";
  "Win evaluation method: **Ways**".
- "A win occurs when at least **one P1 symbol** appears on each of reels **1, 3, and 5**."
- "**Paytable Multiplier:** 10× bet per way."
- Documented worked example: Reel 1: 2 P1, Reel 3: 1 P1, Reel 5: 1 P1 → "**Total Ways** = 2 × 1
  × 1 = 2 ways"; "**Total Payout** = Total Ways × Symbol Multiplier × Bet base = 2 × 10 × Bet
  base = 20 × Bet base."
- Note P1 is one of only two symbols Wild cannot substitute for (the other is FreeSpin).

**Reel Glow** (Features §5) — an anticipation effect with two documented cases.

- Case 1: "If Bonus icon (Scatter type) appears 2 times on different reels, the Reel Glow effect
  will display on the other reels."
- Case 2: "If Gun (P1 icon Scatter type) appears on reels 1 and 3, the Reel Glow effect will
  display on reel 5" — the *behaviour* stands, but the naming in this sentence is a doc error:
  P1's UI name is **Fuel** and its type is **Normal**, not "Gun"/"Scatter type" `[QA-CONFIRMED]`.
- "Gun only Appear in Reel 1,3 and 5" — consistent with P1's reel restriction.

**Multiple Distribution List** (Features §7) — "The game supports the Multiple symbol
distribution list."

- "**Main Game**: When Ante Bet is activated, Use List 3 as Main Game Symbol List"
- "**FreeSpin**: When Ante Bet is activated, Use List 3 as Bonus Game A Symbol List"
- Reselection thresholds:

| Mode | Rules for re-selecting the list | Description |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Main Game** | Threshold = 1 | Each spin → select a new list |
| **FreeSpin** | Threshold = 1 + Every Spin Reselect | Each spin → select a new list |

**Win Tier Multiplier Table** (Features §8) — tiers are ratios of win to bet:

| Win Tier | Condition (Multiplier Range) |
| ----- | :---: |
| **Slim Win** | 0x < win < 1x |
| **Small Win** | 1x ≤ win < 2.5x |
| **Medium Win** | 2.5x ≤ win < 6x |
| **Big Win** | 6x ≤ win < 15x |
| **Mega Win** | 15x ≤ win < 40x |
| **Ultra Win** | win ≥ 40x |

The doc does not state whether "win" is measured against *base bet* or *bet amount* — a live
distinction under Ante Bet (see §5). Big-win celebrations are skipped via `skipBigWin()`, "a
**tap-anywhere Canvas listener**, not a button, so it has no rect".

**Turbo** — `HAS_TURBO | true | btn_turbo`; also `setting:turbo` and `setting:turboTab`;
`getSpeedMode()` is available. Bridge-only evidence; the design doc does not mention turbo.

**History** — `HAS_HISTORY | true`; `getHistoryRows()`, `getHistoryDetailState()`; controls
`history:` `close` `timeRange` `row:<i>` ("virtual list — only rendered rows exist") and
`historyDetail:` `prev` `next` `close`. Note the bridge caveat: `getHistoryRows()` "returns rows
**in a build**" — it needs a real minified build, not editor preview.

**Tutorial WebView** — `HAS_TUTORIAL_WEBVIEW | true`. "The tutorial is a **cross-origin**
`WebView` served from the static host, so injected JS cannot reach inside it. The bridge reports
its page rect only — the framework does the wheeling" (`getTutorialRect()`).

**Promo** — `HAS_PROMO | runtime | true only when the server sent gameInfo.promo`. Popups
`promoList`, `promoReceive`, `promoMessage`. **In QA scope** `[QA-CONFIRMED]`. Testing caveat
from the bridge: the promo popups are "**Promise-driven**: their awaiting flow only resolves when
a specific button fires… hiding alone would strand the flow and hang the game. They close on a
tween, so a single `dismissPopups()` pass can legitimately miss them."

**Replay Mode** — `IS_REPLAY_MODE | runtime | ?mode=replay`; popups `replay`, `replayLanguage`.
**In QA scope** `[QA-CONFIRMED]`.

**Forced Result (test affordance, not a game mechanic)** — `HAS_FORCED_RESULT | true`.
`setForcedResult(result | null)` takes "the **inner** `SpinResult`, not the socket envelope",
travels the game's existing forced-result path, and is **one-shot**.

## 5. Known Limitations and Out-of-Scope Items

**Documentation defects — QA-corrected, recorded so they are not silently re-introduced:**

1. **Free-spin award — RESOLVED by a real payload, and the doc is wrong twice.** The award is
   `base(reels) x ways`, where reels = distinct reels holding a Bonus (12/15/20 for 3/4/5) and
   ways = the product of the per-reel Bonus counts. Verified: `[1,2,0,2,0]` -> 12 x 4 = **48**,
   which is what the server awarded. The doc's "20, 15 and 10" per symbol count is wrong, and its
   "Base Free Spins (3 reels): 15" is wrong (it is 12) — so its worked `15 x 4 = 60` should be
   `12 x 4 = 48`. Full working in `games/chaos/bet-response.md` §6.
2. **The trigger is 3+ distinct REELS, not 3+ symbols** — a distinction neither doc makes.
3. **Ante Bet multiplier.** Game Rules state ante bet "increases the bet by 1.5x". A real
   payload reports `anteMultiplier: 3` with `stake: 1.8` on `baseStake: 0.6`, so **3x is
   correct and the 1.5x text is wrong** `[VERIFIED]`. The same payload proves the payout base
   is untouched by ante — see §4.
4. **Bet-per-line divisor.** Both worked examples use "Bet per line = base bet/5 = 1/5 = $0.2";
   Game Rules state "Per Line winnings: (base bet / 20) × Multiplier". QA confirmed **/20**.
   Consequence: the doc's worked payout figures ("3 of Kind P2 pays = IconMulti*BetPerLine =
   200*0.2 = $40") were computed with the wrong divisor and **do not hold**. Recompute from
   base bet / 20 before asserting any payout.
5. **P1 naming/type in Reel Glow Case 2.** The doc calls P1 "Gun (P1 icon Scatter type)". QA
   confirmed P1 is **Fuel** and **Normal** type. The Reel Glow behaviour itself is unaffected.
6. **Features numbering skips §6** (jumps from §5 Reel Glow to §7 Multiple Distribution List).
   QA confirmed this is **not** a dropped feature — numbering artefact only.
7. **Title spelling.** The design doc's title line reads "Chao Runner"; the bridge doc and the
   game folder use "Chaos Runner"/`chaos`.

**Not documented in the source corpus (verify before asserting):**

8. **No complete paytable.** Only P2's multiplier (200) and P1's 10×-per-way are documented. P3,
   P4, A, K, Q, J and Wild have no payout values, and no per-match-count (3x/4x/5x) breakdown
   exists for any symbol. See [paytable.md](paytable.md).
9. **Payline count and layout** — see §3. Server-driven; not in the docs.
10. **Free-spin re-trigger award amount.** Re-trigger is confirmed to exist `[QA-CONFIRMED]`,
    but how many spins it grants, and whether the trigger count differs from the base trigger,
    are undocumented.
11. **Win-tier reference base.** Whether the win-tier multiplier compares win against *base bet*
    or *bet amount* is unstated, and the two differ by 3× under Ante Bet.
12. **Auto-spin count options** and the existence of an infinite option are undocumented.
13. **Symbol IDs.** No server-side symbol IDs appear anywhere; the STT column is a row index.
14. **Free-spin balance / bet-lock / counter semantics** are assumed from
    `shared-mechanisms/free-spin.md`, not documented for this game.

**Out of scope / division of labour:**

15. The bridge deliberately performs **no game-rule verification**: "payouts, win tiers,
    free-spin math and every rule assertion are the QA framework's job, derived from
    `awaitBetResponse()`'s raw `BetResponse`." Assert rules over the raw response, not over
    bridge-computed state.
16. Bridge verification requires a **real minified build**, not editor preview: "Static types
    prove structure; only a running, **minified** build proves the structure matches reality."
17. Raining Wild is **visual only** — it must never be asserted as affecting payout.
